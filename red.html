Classic Red Roses

1750₺

Origin & History

Originating primarily across Central and East Asia, red roses were cultivated as early as 500 BC in ancient Persian and Chinese royal estates. Today, they stand as the worldwide symbol for deep affection and passion.

Harvesting & Handling

Picked in the early morning before sunlight accelerates transpiration. Buds are inspected for firm, velvety petals and preserved in temperature-controlled coolers at 2°C to 4°C to keep petals crisp and vivid.
