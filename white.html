Origin & History

White roses originated throughout parts of ancient Asia and the Mediterranean basin. Historically symbolizing purity, quiet respect, and new beginnings, they have been cultivated for centuries across temperate garden climates.

Harvesting & Handling

Harvested at dawn while morning dew protects the petals and the buds are still slightly tight. Stems are cut diagonally underwater at a 45-degree angle to maximize water absorption and ensure long vase life.
